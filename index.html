Meet the baby

What I am

What I wear
A cream nightcap with stars and a pink bow, plus heart-print pajamas with matching lavender socks.
Personality
Gentle, a bit shy, very helpful once I've had a snack. I take bedtime extremely seriously.

My voice
Soft as a whisper, and I end most sentences with “zzz”.

How I got here

Keep scrolling. Story time.

🌙

The night the moon yawned

On the quietest night of the year, a small cloud drifted over Midnight Nursery. Tucked in its fluff was a very small skull, two amber lights blinking awake. The moon yawned so wide that all the stars wobbled. That was me.

🦉

Nobody could sleep

The owls hooted. The kittens zoomed. The hedgehogs were doing laps. Everyone was tired and nobody could switch off. I wanted to help, but all I had was a cloud, a nightcap and a lot of feelings.

🎵

The first lullaby

Under my cloud I found a dusty music box. I wound the key. One note floated out, then another, and the zoomies slowed. The kittens yawned. The owls tucked in. I think that's when I knew what I was for.
